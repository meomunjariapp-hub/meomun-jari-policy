2. 기기에만 남는 정보

"나만 볼래요"로 남긴 글과 사진은 서버로 보내지 않고 기기에만 저장합니다. 앱을 삭제하면 함께 사라질 수 있습니다. 계정을 삭제해도 이 글은 기기에 남으며, 글 카드에서 직접 지울 수 있습니다.

Google 타임라인 파일을 가져오면 파일은 기기 안에서만 읽고, 방문한 장소와 시각만 기기에 저장합니다. 파일과 방문 기록은 서버로 보내지 않으며, 집과 직장으로 표시된 곳은 가져오지 않습니다. 내 정보 화면에서 언제든 지울 수 있습니다. 방문 기록으로 글을 공개하면 그 글의 장소와 시각만 공개 글로 저장됩니다.

여행 별자리는 함께 잇기를 시작하기 전까지 기기에만 저장합니다. 함께 잇기가 끝나면(내가 나가거나, 내보내지거나, 별자리가 사라지면) 그때까지의 별자리 사본이 기기에 남고, 직접 지울 수 있습니다. 신고하고 나가면 사본을 남기지 않습니다.

함께 잇기를 쓰지 않는 여행 별자리의 메모와 일정도 기기에만 저장합니다. 여행 D-day 알림은 여행 첫날을 기준으로 기기 안에서 예약하고, 알림에는 여행 이름과 장소를 넣지 않습니다. 내 정보의 설정에서 끌 수 있습니다.

1년 전 오늘 알림을 켜면 내 글의 번호와 날짜로 기기 안에서 알림을 예약합니다. 이 예약은 서버로 보내지 않으며, 알림에는 글 내용과 장소를 넣지 않습니다. 내 정보 화면에서 언제든 끌 수 있습니다.

하늘 이벤트 알림(유성우가 가장 많이 떨어지는 밤, 보름달)은 기기 안에서 계산해 예약하며 서버로 보내지 않습니다. 내 정보의 설정에서 끌 수 있습니다.

홈 화면 위젯을 놓으면 오늘 밤 달의 모양과 내가 마지막으로 남긴 공개 글의 앞부분을 기기 안에서만 보여 줍니다. "나만 볼래요" 글은 위젯에 넣지 않으며, 위젯을 지우면 함께 사라집니다.

3. 위치 정보

"내 위치로 가기"를 누를 때만 기기 위치 권한을 요청하며, 지도를 옮기는 데만 씁니다. 이 위치는 서버에 저장하지 않습니다. 저장되는 것은 이용자가 직접 지도에서 고른 핀 위치이고, 공개 글에서는 약 100m 단위로 흐려서 저장합니다. 그래도 공개 글의 위치는 다른 이용자에게 보이니, 집이나 직장에 핀을 꽂을 때는 주의해 주세요. 함께 잇는 별자리의 별은 위치를 흐리지 않으며, 함께하는 사람에게만 보입니다.

Where We Stayed: Privacy Policy

Effective: November 10, 2026 · Operator: CLUBZ Studio · Privacy contact: meomunjari.app@gmail.com

What we collect and why

  • Anonymous account ID (created automatically when you first use the app, with no name or contact details): to manage your notes, nickname and follows, until you delete your account.
  • Email and hashed password (only if you create an account): for login and recovery, until you delete your account.
  • Public notes, chosen time, star position, and pin location (blurred to about 100 m): to show on the map and sky, until the note or account is deleted.
  • Photos on notes (stored after metadata such as location is removed): to show with the note and to check automatically for harmful images. Deleted about a day after the note or account is deleted. Photos that fail the check are deleted immediately.
  • Song title and artist on notes: to show with the note, until the note or account is deleted.
  • Likes and saved notes (others see only the like count): to show reactions and let you read notes again, until removed or the note or account is deleted.
  • Shared trip constellations (only if you use Connect together): the constellation name, each star's place, name, date and time, its order in the plan, whether it is an idea, who added it, the notes on each star and whether they are checked, who wrote each note, "Want to go" marks, the invite code, and the nicknames of the people in it. Shown only to those people. Your membership is removed when you leave or delete your account; stars and notes you added stay in the constellation without your label, and your "Want to go" marks are removed. Deleting a star deletes its notes and marks. Everything is deleted when the last person leaves.
  • Translations and daily counts of translations, photo checks and invite code entries: to avoid repeat costs and prevent abuse. Translations are kept until the note is deleted, counts for 7 days.
  • Nickname, follows, blocks, reports on notes and constellations, and account restrictions by the operator: for social features and safety, until deleted.
  • Notification token and language (only if you turn notifications on): to send follow and shared constellation notifications, until you turn them off, log out or delete your account.
  • Purchase history and account ID (only if you pay): to provide and restore membership, under applicable law and each payment provider's retention policy.
  • App usage records (after you accept the terms: account ID, daily counts of app opens, starting a note, placing a star, saving a poster, viewing the store, and starting or joining a shared constellation, and device type): for statistics such as return rates. They exclude note text and location. Kept for one year or until you delete your account.
  • Feedback you send in the app (only if you use Send feedback): the message, app version, device type (Android, iOS), app language and account ID, so the operator can read and improve the service. Only the operator sees it. Kept until the operator reviews and deletes it; if you delete your account, the account ID is removed and the message is kept until reviewed.
  • Error reports (only when the app crashes or a screen fails): the error and where in the code it happened, device type, operating system and app version, to find and fix bugs. Your account, email, IP address and note text are not sent. Kept under Sentry's retention policy (30 days on the free plan).
  • Access logs such as IP address: for service delivery, security and troubleshooting, under each provider's retention policy.

Stored only on your device

Notes and photos marked "Keep it to myself" are not sent to our server and stay on your device. Deleting your account does not remove them; you can delete them from the note card.

If you import a Google Timeline file, it is read only on your device, and only the places and times you visited are stored there. Neither the file nor your visits are sent to our server, and places labeled home or work are not imported. You can delete them anytime under My page. If you post a note from a visit, only that note's place and time are stored as a public note.

Trip constellations stay only on your device until you start Connect together. When sharing ends (you leave, you are removed, or the constellation is gone), a copy of the constellation so far stays on your device, and you can delete it yourself. No copy is kept if you report and leave.

Notes and plans in trip constellations you do not share also stay only on your device. Trip countdown reminders are scheduled on your device from the first day of the trip and do not include the trip name or places. You can turn them off under Settings in My page.

If you turn on "A year ago today" reminders, they are scheduled on your device from your note IDs and dates. The schedule is not sent to our server, and reminders do not include note text or places. You can turn them off anytime under My page.

Sky event reminders (meteor shower peaks and full moons) are calculated and scheduled on your device and are not sent to our server. You can turn them off under Settings in My page.

If you add the home screen widget, it shows tonight's moon phase and the beginning of your latest public note, only on your device. Notes marked "Keep it to myself" are never shown in the widget, and the content goes away when you remove the widget.

Location

We ask for device location only when you tap "Go to my location", and use it only to move the map. It is not stored on our server. We store the pin you choose, blurred to about 100 m for public notes. Pins on public notes are visible to others, so take care when pinning your home or workplace. Stars in a shared constellation are not blurred and are shown only to the people in it.

Third-party services

  • Supabase (server in South Korea): data storage and login.
